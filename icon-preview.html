💡 Design Philosophy

Symbolism

  • Lotus Petals: Universal symbol of meditation and mindfulness
  • Three-fold pattern: Mind-Body-Spirit or Past-Present-Future
  • Center dot: Focal point of awareness and practice
  • Outer ring: Containment and mindful boundaries

Color Choice

  • Saffron (#b45309): Deep turmeric shade with cultural significance
  • Warm aesthetic: Matches the app's "calm & mindful" design language
  • High contrast: Ensures legibility at all sizes (4.5:1+ ratio)

Technical Excellence

  • Simple geometry: Recognizable from 16×16 to 512×512
  • 4×4 supersampling: Smooth edges without anti-aliasing artifacts
  • Zero dependencies: Generated entirely with Node.js built-ins
  • Platform-optimized: Separate scales for standard, maskable, and iOS
